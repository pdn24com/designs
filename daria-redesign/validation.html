Experiments and hypotheses

E1 · Faces vs chips

Hypothesis: a tabbed Up next face is found as quickly as a labelled “Queue” chip and is preferred for reordering because the transport stays visible. Measure: time to first queue interaction in V2 between two prototype variants; preference after both. Decision rule: if the chip variant is found more than 5 s faster on average, add a Queue chip on the Cover face that switches faces.

E2 · Resume card

Hypothesis: repeat listeners do not miss a home page because the mini player carries the last session. Measure: in a diary-style follow-up, what participants do in the first 10 s of opening the app. Decision rule: if more than half start by opening the player or the Recently played tile, promote a resume card to the top of Songs (no architecture change).

E3 · Stage mode

Hypothesis: a persistent player with a library sheet (Concept C3) is preferred for casual listening but rejected for library work. Measure: V1, V2 and V4 in Stage vs Dock; preference by library size. Decision rule: if Stage wins on preference for small libraries without losing V4, offer it as an optional player layout.

E4 · Immediate settings vs draft

Hypothesis: immediate switches with Undo produce fewer errors and less hesitation than Apply/Discard for folder hiding. Measure: hesitation before toggling; unintended hides; recovery time. Decision rule: if unintended hides are not recovered within 10 s by most participants, add a confirmation for folders over 100 songs.

E5 · Lyrics face before lyrics exist

Hypothesis: the empty Lyrics face with its explanation reads as “this song has no lyrics”, not “the app is broken”. Measure: think-aloud on first opening the face. Decision rule: any participant calling it broken means the face ships behind a flag until lyrics exist.

E6 · Daria tile

Hypothesis: generated tiles are not mistaken for real artwork and make grids of missing-art albums scannable. Measure: ask which albums have real covers; time to find a named album in the Albums grid. Decision rule: if tiles are mistaken for art, add the note glyph at all sizes and a “no cover” line in Song info (already present).

Technical checks (engineering, not participants)

  • Dock blur cost on the lowest-end supported device; frame time while scrolling Songs with the mini player playing. Decision rule: solid dock by default on devices that drop frames.
  • Tint extraction per cover ≤ 20 ms on a background thread with a cache keyed by file identity.
  • Folder-hide recount time for 825 and 5,000 tracks; decision rule for the draft fallback is 300 ms.
  • Contrast of text on the ten tints in light and dark, plus the fallback, in an automated check.
  • Landscape player on the phone that reported clipping.
  • Timer expiry, +10 cap, change-while-running, expiry-while-editing, save-failure Retry, reboot discard, and end-of-song retargeting through an external controller, on device. These are contract checks the redesign must not regress.
  • Editor: consent denial, external change, interrupted write with restore, low space, second save while busy, on an MP3 corpus that includes Persian tags and ID3v2.3/2.4 mixes.
